  - Kotak panah kuning: Managing Risk Through Operation
  - Kotak biru di kanan atas (Bussiness Process): 
    - Front End
    - Middle End
    - Back End
    - Keterangan di bawah kotak biru: Risk Mangement through daily business operations

  - Kotak panah kuning: Managing Risk Through Capital
  - Kotak biru di kanan bawah (Capital Management & Planning):

    - Keterangan di bawah kotak biru: Capital as buffer for unexpected loss; capital as risk-return allocation
